# Product Specification

Web3 opportunity claims are distributed across sources of uneven quality. Dropchoice helps users see evidence, uncertainty, effort, costs, deadlines, and safety risks in a research workspace.

Goal: support informed research without representing uncertain outcomes as guaranteed or executing financial actions.

User jobs: discover signals; inspect evidence; understand unknowns; track tasks; compare hypothetical routes; identify safety warnings and stale sources.

Surfaces: Home Feed, Opportunities, read-only Action Preview, Events, My Activity, Safety & Alerts.

"Worth researching", "Proceed with caution", and "Avoid" are editorial classifications, not financial advice, forecasts, or eligibility/reward guarantees.

Current limits: seeded local data and client-side state only; no live ingestion, authentication, durable database, wallet connection, signatures, or transaction execution.

Before production define measures for evidence completeness/freshness, traceable evaluations, warning visibility, task completion, accessibility, and performance.

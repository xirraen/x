# Social platform icons

The social card uses only five profile links supplied for this preview: Twitter/X, Bluesky, Farcaster, Hey.xyz, and UpScrolled.

- `x.svg`, `bluesky.svg`, `farcaster.svg`: copied unchanged from Simple Icons v16.34.0 (https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons; https://www.npmjs.com/package/simple-icons). The selected icon entries do not include per-icon license metadata; brand marks remain subject to their owners' rights and guidelines. No blanket license claim is made for these marks.
- `hey.png`: Hey.xyz app icon shown by the Ethereum.org Hey.xyz app listing (https://ethereum.org/apps/heyxyz/).
- `upscrolled.svg`: icon served by UpScrolled's own site at https://upscrolled.com/icon.svg.

These marks identify their respective platforms and do not imply affiliation or endorsement. The five profile URLs are taken exactly from the user's request.
